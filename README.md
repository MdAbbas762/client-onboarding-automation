# 🚀 Client Onboarding Automation

Automate the client onboarding process by creating a personalized Google Sheets workspace from a Google Form submission and automatically sharing it with the client via email.

---

## 📌 Overview

This automation streamlines the client onboarding process by eliminating repetitive manual tasks. When a client submits a Google Form, Zapier automatically creates a copy of a predefined Google Sheets template, populates it with the submitted information, and sends a confirmation email notifying the client that their information has been successfully received and their dedicated spreadsheet has been created.

The workflow ensures a consistent onboarding process while reducing manual effort and the possibility of human error.

## ✨ Features

- Automatically triggers on every new Google Form submission.
- Creates a dedicated copy of a Google Sheets template.
- Populates the spreadsheet with the client's submitted information.
- Sends an automated confirmation email after the client's personalized spreadsheet is created.
- Eliminates repetitive manual onboarding tasks.

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

1. A client submits a Google Form.
2. Zapier creates a copy of a predefined Google Sheets template.
3. The copied spreadsheet is populated with the client's submitted information.
4. A confirmation email is sent to the client informing them that their information has been received and their personalized spreadsheet has been created.
